Privacy & storage

Your PC stays in this browser only. Use Export to carry an encrypted copy to another device.

To restore on another device, paste this ID there. The server only ever stores ciphertext.

Backups and exports use AES-256-GCM with a key derived from your passphrase (PBKDF2-SHA256, 310k iterations). Storage hosts only ever see ciphertext.
